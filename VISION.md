# Vision

Football IQ AI does not publish unexplained predictions.

Project Athena transforms football facts, context, evidence, counter-evidence,
risk, and uncertainty into a clear verdict that users can understand.

## Foundational principles

1. WHY comes before WHAT.
2. Context matters as much as statistics.
3. The system must challenge its own conclusion.
4. Every factor must be measurable, explainable, and historically testable.
5. Teams evolve through managers, transfers, injuries, tactics, and time.
